# Step 4 implementation notes — pending, not implemented

The accepted roadmap is authoritative. Keep player and bot consequences in the same CPU state, with versioned blueprint/catalog data and explicit provisional calibration. This note narrows the next work without claiming historical crew, pump or ammunition performance.

- Add HE alongside AP, with finite separate stocks and a loading state that prevents instant ammunition swaps from bypassing reloads. Preserve total ammunition accounting. HE bursts on physical contact; its local fragments/heat use the step-3 protection queries. Shared nominal gun flight parameters may initially be retained as an explicit charge-envelope approximation. Calibrate HE for exposed/lightly protected equipment; it must not bypass heavy armor.
- Track local heat, finite combustible load and fire intensity for compartments and gun mounts. Delivered burst damage can ignite those targets; nearby armor and closed boundaries constrain propagation. Water suppresses fire. Fire damage persists after extinguishing, and destroyed equipment cannot recover simply by cooling or draining.
- Replace magazine HP-zero explosions with ignition and protection behavior. Directly reached ammunition and sustained fire are threats; flooded ammunition must not detonate by the old HP trigger. Mount-to-magazine links can support a bounded flash path with explicit protection. Do not turn every gunhouse fire into an automatic remote explosion.
- Start damage control with authored team counts and simple setup/work timers. Priorities: balanced, fires, flooding, repairs. Flooding work covers accessible small-hole shoring, closing intact open boundaries and portable pumping; damaged bulkheads cannot be magically closed. Repairs use finite spares, restore only a limited fraction of damaged equipment, and never rebuild destroyed guns/engines or replace ammunition. Automatic crews use the same rules for bots.
- Expose selected ammunition, stock/loading state, active fires, assigned teams and remaining repair resources in the existing compact naval instruments. Explain actions in terms of their consequence; keep the ship and sea visible. Include a useful focus choice if automatic priorities alone leave the player unable to direct help to threatened machinery.

Acceptance scenarios: HE harms an exposed target more usefully than an unarmed AP over-penetration; heavy protection blocks HE; changing shell type consumes a load interval and never creates stock; a contained fire dies when fought or its fuel is exhausted; neglect can disable equipment; wet magazines survive the same thermal insult that ignites a dry unprotected magazine; pump/isolation priority changes water transfer; repairs consume resources and cannot revive HP-zero equipment. Reset and frame-rate determinism cover all new state. Relevant full tests/build, live controls, all required asset reviews, and Fable review after each bounded slice remain required.
