## Verified
- **Ordered contact/time splitting** (`advanceProjectile`): each tick is resolved contact by contact; the chord fraction is mapped to elapsed time under linear drag (`−log1p(−k·t·F)/k`, exact for the drag law, chord sagitta < 1 mm per tick); speed, budget, age and remaining time are updated at each contact, so residual speed and fuze delay change travel inside the same tick (test). Multi-ship nearest-contact selection is correct. The 64-iteration cap is far from reach: a bow-on inert through-shot along the whole hull logs 7 contacts, max 1 per tick.
- **Residual speed** (`pay`): `v ← v·(residual/before)^(1/1.4)` keeps speed and budget on the same curve (my 3c recommendation); a stop zeroes velocity. Module contact costs 50 mm and slows the shell ~8 % at 468 mm budget.
- **Arming/delay**: arms on the first non-ricochet contact with resistance ≥ caliber/6 (38 cm: 63 mm), including a contact that stops the shell (armed stop lodges). Delay is honoured exactly within ticks; a lodged shell follows the hull (ship-local or mount-local) and bursts on time (tests). Bow plating 60 mm at normal incidence does not arm the 38 cm, so thin ends over-penetrate unarmed; end closures 20 mm never arm. Sea contact never arms.
- **Turret continuation**: mounted plates no longer terminate; the shell pays entry and exit plates, damages the mount once per shell (`mount-damage` key), and can carry into the barbette. Probe: 38 cm into Bruno's side at 805 m/s → penetrated, armed, exit 273 m/s, burst inside the gunhouse 35 ms later damaging the mount. Legacy box mounts get entry/exit keys with correct exit normals.
- **End-to-end citadel burst**: synthetic 800 mm shell at y = 1.5 m → belt, teak, support, turtleback, partition → armed → bursts inside the forward boiler room, damaging two boiler rooms and five watertight boundaries. A shell stopped by armour bursts outside it without transmitting damage through it (test and probe: stop at Anton barbette → burst, magazine untouched).
- **Bounded blast/fragments**: one nearest-point ray per module/mount/boundary within `clamp(3·∛kg, 0.5, 15)` m (38 cm 8.1 m, 46 cm 10 m, 15 cm 3.1 m); rays pay every intervening layer with a fresh visited set; any steel sets fragments-only exposure (35 % of remaining budget); torpedo bulkhead 45 Ww > 30 mm fragment budget blocks, 5 mm partitions do not. Magazine consequence shared through `checkMagazine` for contact and burst.
- **Data**: `ap` validated (delay 1–200 ms, filler < projectile mass, budgets bounded); recipe deterministic from caliber/mass; small AA inert; basis text states provisional calibration. HUD shows exit speed and fuze state; burst VFX/audio keyed on `burst` + `detonation`.
- **Stock outcomes (seed 11)**: 850 m 201 s / 0.8 m³ (68 bursts); 5 km 366 s / 129 m³ / 1.2° list (75 bursts, 8 module events; 3c: 346 s / 105 m³); 15 km still no sink in 25 min but 192 m³, 2 mounts destroyed, 44 module events (3c 154 m³); Baltimore stern 5 km 313 s / 78 m³ with 1 burst (19 mm plating does not arm the 8-inch at normal incidence — over-penetration, plausible); Yamato vs Bismarck 12 km 768 s / 359 m³ (73 bursts). Defeat still `structural-fallback` everywhere (step 5).

## P1 · Burst work is unbounded in practice and spikes the tick — request change before merge
Each burst ray runs a full `shipContacts` query (743 plates broad-phased, 360 boundary boxes, mounts with transforms): 114 µs for a 5 m ray on Bismarck. A port-wing burst has 24 targets within 8.1 m → 2.7 ms; measured 2.2–3.9 ms per burst (0.6–0.9 ms outside the hull or in a turret). 5v5 mixed fleet, 60 s: mean 1.8 ms, p50 1.6, **p99 4.3 ms, max 13.6 ms**, 138 ticks with bursts, up to 3 bursts in one tick (contention-inflated, but the ratio to the 1.2–1.4 ms base is the point). With rendering on the same thread this will hitch whenever a salvo arms. Concrete fix: build one per-burst candidate list (plates, boundaries, modules, mounts whose bounding box intersects the burst sphere) and run rays only against it, or pass a candidate filter into `shipContacts`; optionally cap rays at the 32 nearest targets. Expected ≈ 0.1–0.3 ms per burst. Deterministic and local to `burst.ts`.

## Nits / accepted approximations (no change needed for 3d)
- Armed shells that leave the hull below the waterline or strike open sea end as `passed-through`/`splash` without bursting; underwater bursts next to a hull (flooding) are not modelled. Note for step 5.
- Bursts inside a gunhouse cannot reach the magazine (≈12 m below, radius 8 m); only a shell physically continuing down the barbette can. Flash-down/ignition is step 4, as planned.
- A stopped AP shell now always produces a burst event at the armour face 35 ms later (VFX flash, no damage). Visually plausible; the ledger shows it as a terminal burst — fine, but "AP shell burst" vs "burst against armour" wording would help.
- 'expired' from the 64-iteration cap is silent; a counter in diagnostics would make a pathological definition visible.
- AP contact damage is 25 % of `damage` (17.5 for 38 cm), so magazines now need 6 contacts or 2 bursts; consistent with retaining HP detonation until step 4.
- `burstShell` iterates all actors including the owner; harmless.

## Scope check
Legacy inert parts keep contact-only behaviour plus residual speed; `hitShip` retained as the controlled through-shot API; determinism preserved (fleet fps test passes); no blueprint or model changes beyond the catalog `ap` block.
