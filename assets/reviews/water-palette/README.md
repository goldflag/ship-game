# Water palette and horizon review

**Follow-up decision:** the horizon softening from PR #66 was reverted at the user’s request. The naval-blue water palette, wave tuning and brighter port lighting remain. The extra sky/water haze, cool-color sampler overrides and earlier cloud fade are removed, and battle fog returns to power 1.4 with each map/weather’s full authored sky-blend distance. The screenshots and implementation notes below are historical evidence of PR #66, before this horizon revert.

The user supplied the Fort Worth and Lüshun B screenshots in `references/` as the final water direction: deep navy blue, cool glints, small crossing waves, and a soft blue horizon. These are user-provided World of Warships / Wargaming screenshots used only as visual references; no geometry or textures are copied from them.

**Decision: A · Naval blue was selected for implementation.** The game uses deep navy-blue water with small regional shifts, cool crest transmission and a broader ripple spectrum. The values live in `assets/maps/environments.v1.json`, the weather amplitudes in `assets/maps/battle-conditions.v1.json`, and the lighting/spectrum setup in `src/game/Game.ts`. The original PR also included `src/game/HorizonHaze.ts`, which the horizon revert removes. The temporary palette route, switching code and rejected B/C captures have been removed.

Run `bun run dev` and open `/` to play with the accepted treatment. Open [the historical before/after comparison](index.html) for all four maps and the port.

The 24 retained images were captured through Orca's embedded browser using the real Game renderer during the palette review. They are 1600 × 900, WebGPU, High quality, pixel ratio 1, ACES, exposure 1, paused Bismarck at water tick 3600. Camera and applied colors are recorded in `captures.json`; each change settled for 32 serialized frames. WebP quality is 0.92, with no retouching or color correction. Temporal clouds can vary slightly between captures. The baseline is `716c34f787ceaf658a2fe425da73e100f5a2ab45`; its map and weather settings are preserved in `baseline-environments.v1.json` and `baseline-conditions.v1.json`. The capture prototype was removed after selection; these images document the accepted appearance before the upstream grazing-angle shader patch. [Final port capture](port-final.png) shows the normal game after updating to master, with sun intensity 5.8 and ambient fill 1.75 verified through the live diagnostics.

Port sun intensity rises from 5 to 5.8 and hemisphere fill from 1.1 to 1.75. Sun direction, sky exposure and the restrained port sun glare settings remain fixed. Water absorption coefficients and the existing submerged-camera attenuation remain unchanged.

The original PR #66 treatment softened the horizon on every map. `HorizonHaze.ts` takes live sky radiance just above the aerosol band and gives it a cool gray-blue chroma. A smooth angular blend joins the sky, reflections and distant water across roughly 2.6 degrees. Water gets the additional blend between 250 and 1,800 m at grazing angles, only above the surface; ships, terrain and transparent combat effects retain their own materials. A far-plane veil finishes the sky after the cloud composite; the water blend handles its premultiplied transparency to avoid a color seam. Cloud melt is 16–32 km, and fog starts blending toward sky color earlier without shortening the configured sight-distance endpoint. No screen-space blur or depth post-fog is added. The broader wave spectrum uses spectral sharpness 0.8 and JONSWAP gamma 2.2, with choppiness 0.65. Clear/Fog, Moderate, Overcast and Storm amplitudes are 0.12 / 0.24 / 0.34 / 0.48; port amplitude stays 0.12. The original captures use the prior spectrum, choppiness and amplitudes from `baseline-conditions.v1.json`. Wind speed, wavelengths and CPU ship motion remain unchanged.

The removed horizon implementation used Sky Pro 2.2's documented angular LUT mapping and a narrow adapter to Water Pro 3.5.1's private `waterMaterial.outputNode`; those bindings are no longer part of the game. Quality changes in the game recreate the complete Game renderer. This is artistic tuning, not a measured atmosphere or regional water model.

Validation after updating to master `f927cd10`: the 34 relevant battle, landscape, environment and Game frame tests pass. `bun run build` passes, including all ship/aircraft checks, TypeScript and the Vite production bundle. The earlier missing comparison ZIP blocker was resolved by the upstream pipeline update; no ship authoring inputs or generated ship binaries were changed here.
