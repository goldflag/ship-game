How the maps play

Each coastal map keeps a clear shipping lane for the two fleets. Larger rosters widen the lane automatically. Coastlines appear on the navigation chart; ships stop at shore and can reverse away. Bots steer away from nearby land. Low shells and torpedoes hit the coast, while shells with enough elevation can pass over it.

Sea conditions adjust the selected map’s waves. Returning to port restores the sheltered harbor and its daylight. These are original, region-inspired game landscapes, not replicas of surveyed places.

Atmospheres and collision envelopes are artistic gameplay approximations. Grounding damage, tides, detailed seabed simulation, and global bot route planning are not implemented.
