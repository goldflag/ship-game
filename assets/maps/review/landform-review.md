# Landform visual review

September 6, 2026. Fresh visual review of the final game captures and desktop/mobile screenshot document.

**Verdict: pass for the game's viewing scale.** All nine landforms were inspected. The result supports more realistic-looking landforms, without implying photorealism or surveyed regional geography.

| Finding | Final disposition |
| --- | --- |
| F1 — Arctic surface repetition | Resolved. Smooth snow and irregular surface detail replace the repeated rock pattern. |
| F2 — Tree stamps, lighting, grounding | Resolved at gameplay scale. Muted, varied crowns form coherent forest masses. Tree impostors remain recognizable in close views, without the earlier isolated black stamps or dominant pale speckling. |
| F3 — Extruded coast and transition seam | Resolved. Coastal slopes vary and the Arctic transition seam is removed. |
| F4 — Volcanic flank readability | Resolved. Standard and detail captures retain visible rock, drainage, and woodland under consistent illumination. |
| F5 — Invalid review captures | Resolved for terrain inspection. Correct close cameras and all nine survey views were verified. Aerial surveys explicitly hide cloud volume to avoid a separate cloud-compositor artifact; standard and close views retain map clouds. |

Ships and open water remain clear while mountains provide distinct landmarks. Desktop and mobile document layouts are readable and consistent with the naval styling. Navigation wraps on mobile and comparison images fit their columns. No further material fixes were required for this review's scope.

Final validation: 347 tests passed; production build passed. The five-page before/after PDF contains nine comparison and close-view images, and the six-page ocean guide contains all four current map images.
