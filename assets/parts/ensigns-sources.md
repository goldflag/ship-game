# Period naval ensigns

`ensigns.ts` is an original geometric raster recipe. No museum image is shipped or used as a texture. The selected national designs are distinct from fleet team colors.

- US ships, including the US merchant presets: 48 stars in six rows of eight and thirteen stripes. [US Naval History and Heritage Command, USS Samuel B. Roberts ensign](https://www.history.navy.mil/our-collections/artifacts/ship-and-shore/Flags/NationalFlags/uss-samuel-b-roberts--de-413--ensign-from-leyte-gulf.html).
- Royal Navy and the 1941 Canadian Flower: White Ensign. [Royal Museums Greenwich](https://www.rmg.co.uk/collections/objects/rmgc-object-801), [Royal Canadian Navy historical ensign](https://www.canada.ca/en/navy/corporate/history-heritage/the-canadian-naval-ensign.html).
- Imperial Japanese Navy: hoist-offset sun disc with sixteen rays. [Royal Museums Greenwich, naval ensign 1889–1945](https://www.rmg.co.uk/collections/objects/rmgc-object-487).
- German warships: 1935–1945 naval ensign, including its period central emblem and canton Iron Cross. [Royal Museums Greenwich](https://www.rmg.co.uk/collections/objects/rmgc-object-479). Type VIIC is presented in its original German service identity, rather than the captured vessel's temporary British colors.

Colors are calibrated for the game's daylight, not measured fabric dyes. Small emblem outlines, flag dimensions, and positions are approximations. The single ensign remains hoisted in port and underway; national jacks, commissioning pennants, signal flags, flag etiquette, battle damage and hoisting/lowering are not represented. The cloth is a visual constrained-particle model with aerodynamic pressure, drag and gravity, not a validated textile or fluid simulation.
