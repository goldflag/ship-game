# Kongō 356 mm twin turret

The reusable turret includes its shallow annular roller bearing, with a support
attachment at runtime Y = 3.14 m in the retained joint coordinate system.
The Shipbuilder owns the adjustable barbette below that plane; the original
fixed cylinder is omitted. Joint, muzzle and canvas-cover datums are unchanged.

Two open recoil wells in the working floor continue into the installation's
hollow barbette. Their declared apertures match the original mesh; there are no
fixed pan bottoms to obstruct the guns at elevation and full recoil. Supporting
decks, working wells and installation clearance remain the ship's responsibility.
