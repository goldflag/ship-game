# Kongō 356 mm twin turret

The reusable turret includes its shallow annular roller bearing, with a support
attachment at runtime Y = 3.14 m in the retained joint coordinate system.
The Shipbuilder owns the adjustable barbette below that plane; the original
fixed cylinder is omitted. Joint, muzzle and canvas-cover datums are unchanged.

Two open recoil wells in the working floor continue into the installation's
hollow barbette. Their declared apertures match the original mesh; there are no
fixed pan bottoms to obstruct the guns at elevation and full recoil. Supporting
decks, working wells and installation clearance remain the ship's responsibility.

The catalog comparison uses the registered JGM049 non-rangefinder form. Its
identity is **related variant**: the exact 1944 source fit is unresolved, and the
retained optional rangefinder assembly is not certified by that comparison.
The muzzle/chase diameters and the soft canvas shoulder now follow that source
more closely. Supported shoulder guardrails recover its visible roof outline.
Continuous four-sided collar seams replace separately capped short rods,
reallocating triangles to those rails without decimation. The full standalone
export is 6,788 triangles, below the previous merged 7,420-triangle model.

The longer retained barrels/muzzle datums, afterbody curvature, sight hoods and
optional rangefinder fit remain approximations. The shallow bearing, Y=3.14 m
attachment, two open recoil wells, articulation IDs and ballistics are unchanged.
