# US main-battery turrets

Original Python recipes preserve the Iowa Mk 7 and Baltimore Mk 12 identities,
articulation joints and muzzle sockets. The approved comparison resources are
`agm034_16in50_mk7` and `agm019_8in55_ca68` in the component reference register;
external geometry is viewing-only.

Canvas seams follow the original sloped face and remain fixed to the gunhouse;
low-resolution elevation morphs move the collar while the barrel recoils through
its cylindrical sleeve. The radial and forward fullness are deliberate visual
approximations, not cloth simulation. Main tubes use sixteen radial sides.
Iowa rangefinder ears are narrower and Baltimore retains its prominent aft sight
mast. Fine roof seams and small rangefinder bolts are omitted; ladders, optics,
handrails and access fittings remain.

Standalone review is not historical certification or installed ship clearance.
These remain independently authored approximations, including the rangefinder
hood sections and simplified fabric folds.
