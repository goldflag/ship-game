# US main-battery turrets

Original Python recipes preserve the Iowa Mk 7 and Baltimore Mk 12 identities,
articulation joints and muzzle sockets. The approved comparison resources are
`agm034_16in50_mk7` and `agm019_8in55_ca68` in the component reference register;
external geometry is viewing-only.

Iowa follows the registered Mk 7 resource's clear roof and bare aft sides. Its
previous projecting rangefinder ears, roof periscopes/rails and rear baskets are
omitted because they are absent from that resource; this is a source-configuration
choice, not a historical correction or a claim about every Iowa turret/refit.
The forward sight housings have chamfered profiles. The main tubes retain sixteen
radial sides and a full-length cylindrical recoil sleeve, then taper continuously
instead of carrying the previous abrupt external shoulder.

Canvas seams follow the sloped face and remain fixed to the gunhouse. Iowa's
six-ring covers use a localized raised crown at the roof/face break; all elevation
shapes preserve the fixed seam and sliding cuff. The barrel recoils through that
cuff. Canvas fullness and folds remain visual approximations, not cloth simulation.
Baltimore retains its separate rangefinder hoods, roof fittings and aft sight mast.

Standalone review is not historical certification or installed ship clearance.
Remaining approximations include Iowa's smooth canvas folds versus the coarse
reference facets, simplified sight boxes and omitted shallow rear base recesses.
