# Shielded Oerlikons — approved Hsienyang Hull A fit

Original Mk4 single and paired mount recipes based on inspected [GameModels3D pzsd108](https://gamemodels3d.com/games/worldofwarships/vehicles/pzsd108) close-ups. The source equipment entry calls the pair Mk24; its visual asset name says Mk20. That unresolved identification is retained rather than merging the variant with other fleet Oerlikons.

Foot, tapering pedestal, height wheel, fork, split shields, bracing, receiver, jacket springs, feed drum, trigger handles, shoulder rests and ring sights are independent original geometry. The fleet's barrel layout and yaw/elevation/recoil/socket contract are reused. Neutral geometry is reconstructed from the source's 30° display pose. Ballistics and durability inherit explicitly provisional fleet game values.

The twin's measured source pivot is 0.299 m aft of the pedestal center and 1.174 m above its foot. Its two vertical shoulder cups, separate outboard grips, central sight, feed drums and raked fork are reconstructed around that pivot. Both retained elevation joints receive the same CPU mount elevation; their recoil joints and muzzle sockets remain separate.

The single's source pivot is 1.1505 m high with the same aft offset. It retains its narrower fork, canted height wheel, offset magazine and asymmetric shoulder rests/sight. Original fabric catchers follow the source's hanging silhouette at 30°. Each catcher connects to a fixed carriage frame and a cradle frame, with elevation-driven morphs keeping both seams attached. Cloth folding is a visual approximation; no source geometry is imported.

Under the approved technical gun-stop research exception, [Navy OP 911, pp. 15–17](https://www.maritime.org/doc/gun20mm/index.php) supplies the Mk4 single family's −5°/+87° travel and identifies its stationary barrel/breech casing. The Mk4 Mod. 3 exception is +90°; that modification is not established for this source. [OP 1439, pp. 11–12](https://www.maritime.org/doc/gun20twin/index.php) gives −15°/+85° for the Mk20/Mk24 twins. The two catalog variants now use these family limits and zero external barrel recoil. These values do not establish Hsienyang's installed stop-cam profiles. External geometry and paint still use only the approved GameModels3D source.

The Hsienyang installation uses clearance-derived −9°/+78° gameplay stops for the twin. These are not verified historical limits. The reusable twin remains unsuitable for unrestricted catalog travel: rigid mechanisms contact the stand/shield near full depression and the grip/height wheel above roughly 80°. Other installations must review their own travel and geometry. Existing Iowa and generic single IDs are preserved.

The original single’s exposed bearing caps now clear the receiver, while the retained trunnions provide the mating connection. Its drum lip stays within the inspected faceted envelope; the corrected rigid single clears the sampled −5°/+87° local mechanism sweep, including intermediate angles. Both fabric variants clear the sampled local mechanism sweep at half-degree intervals; the twin rigid mechanism clears its installed −9°/+78° range. Each ship still needs its own installed and neighboring-weapon review.
