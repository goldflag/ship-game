# German cruiser construction equipment

Original reusable fittings adapted from the repository's original
`assets/ships/admiral-hipper/build.py`. The user approved that existing preset as
the construction conversion target. Fresh external-reference verification is
incomplete; these are not independently certified historical variants.

The open funnel cap preserves its swept rim and rain grating. Its datum is the
jacket top; the ship owns the lower uptake and supporting gallery. The triple
533 mm bank retains open launching troughs, breech covers, bands, air flasks,
control shelter and separate yaw/muzzle nodes. It uses canonical G7a ammunition.
Port and starboard 3.4 m screws are distinct handed variants, with independent
spin nodes and forward shaft-seat sockets. The original opposite-hand propeller
approximation remains accepted. The balanced rudder retains its stock and yaw
node. No downloaded or generated model supplies authoring geometry.

Package masses and functional values are provisional game conventions: cap
8.5 t and 82 MW exhaust capacity; each screw 2.9 t and 0.65 efficiency; rudder
5.5 t and 18 m² area; torpedo mount 12 t including three ready torpedoes.
The original generic steam package occupies 12 × 4 × 20 m and declares 82 MW,
2,400 t dry plus 600 t service/fuel allowance. Its visible internals are schematic,
not historical machinery plans. The separate 50,000-round magazine uses the
registered generic rack builder, 8 × 3 × 16 m, 65 t dry. Loaded ammunition mass is
computed by Rust. Construction source declares all additional fixed stores.

Gun variants use their existing registered original builders unchanged. Their
construction catalog envelopes are measured from standalone exports; attachment
sockets preserve their original sole heights. Hipper gun variants have closed
external barbettes in the ship source; their internal hoist wells are not
reconstructed. The rudder uses an explicit stock-bearing attachment above its
lowest blade extent.

The SL8 director retains its sphere, collar and optical tubes. Its conservative
fitting boxes enclose every published triangle while allowing the real curved
housing to sit beside the bridge. The lattice catapult, guarded director optics
and four boat sizes reuse original source builders. Boat datums include their
cradles; the ship supplies the supporting frames.

Standalone publication does not certify installed articulation. Review metadata
remains `unreviewed`; each ship must pass native fitting and exact-model visual
checks. Preview files and diagnostics belong in `.build/`.
