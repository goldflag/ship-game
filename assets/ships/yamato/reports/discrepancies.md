# Yamato discrepancy register

The requested endpoint is a historically accurate Yamato with real dimensions and proportions. **Not achieved.** Passing the asset validator certifies agreement with the authored blueprint, not agreement with every historical feature.

Working fit: 7 April 1945. Working datum: 10.4 m trial draft. This deliberately identifies the load datum independently of the exterior equipment date; the exact departure/sinking draft has not been established.

## Reference conflicts

S-01-3 p.42 reports LOA 259 m and incorrectly describes the main caliber as 45 cm. Kure Museum gives 263 m and 46 cm; O-45(N) independently gives actual caliber 46 cm. The older summary is therefore not used as unqualified proof of all dimensions. Its attached drawing register lists plans that are not included in this 61-page scan.

The Alexpl plan/profile is credited reference art and a modern reconstruction. It is suitable for identifying visible discrepancies, but matching it does not prove the real ship. No GameModels3D or other game mesh/texture has been copied.

S-06-2 p.10 independently confirms 263 m LOA, 256 m waterline length, 36.9 m waterline beam and 10.4 m trial draft, but gives extreme beam 38.8 m. The museum specification summary and Tamiya original instruction table both give 38.9 m. This conflict is retained, not silently described as unanimous primary evidence. The museum's original screw/rudder educational page states a 5 m screw diameter; the initial 6 m estimate has been removed.

The museum director's [bridge reconstruction account](https://yamato-museum.com/note/post_14/) states that surviving drawings differ and that its own reconstruction is not 100%. The museum's [drawing guide](https://yamatomuseum.securesite.jp/et/?page_id=1142) describes the destruction of most originals and the later discovery of bridge drawings. No amount of successful export validation supplies that missing historical evidence.

## Visual review, build b81a8d55e9fd

Profile, plan, quarter, bow and stern inspected. Corrected rear turret roof AA attachment, forefoot faceting, waterline width/length, unsupported stern AA, closed boat bays, screw diameter, rudder outline and single radar array. Bridge galleries, navigation glazing, conning tower, curved funnel uptake, six searchlight stations and rounded boat-bay ends have since been revised from museum references. Remaining visible discrepancies include simplified secondary gunhouses, AA shields, aft director, stern machinery and fittings, plus unverified positions and profiles across the model. Those are open historical/modeling work, not passed items.
