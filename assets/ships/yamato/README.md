# Yamato

7 April 1945 exterior fit; 10.4 m model waterline datum. Original reconstruction refined against the GameModels3D visual configuration; historical accuracy remains unverified.

Open `/?ship=yamato` or select this ship in port or Custom battle.

`blueprint.json`, `build.py` and its region modules are the durable inputs: `yamato_kit.py` holds the shared scene, materials and primitives, and `yamato_tower.py`, `yamato_funnel.py`, `yamato_citadel.py` and `yamato_deck.py` each build one region, in that dependency order after the shelter deck. Reusable equipment comes from `assets/parts/`. `recipe-inputs.json` declares additional original dependencies. Generated Blender scenes and runtime models are build outputs.

## Approved reference brief

The requested refinement targets [GameModels3D Yamato `pjsb018`](https://gamemodels3d.com/en/games/worldofwarships/vehicles/pjsb018), `A_Hull` and its default equipment configuration. Its hull geometry is named `jsb039_yamato_1945`; the source's upgrade label says `Yamato_1944`, so neither label establishes historical accuracy. This pass uses GameModels3D only as an external visual reference and retains the existing Kure-gray appearance. Geometry and textures are authored independently.

Comparison uses 15 metres per source unit and, in Blender's bow-positive coordinates, a longitudinal offset of -2.5 m with no vertical offset. The reference shell spans 262.974 m and reaches -10.404 m; the authored shell remains 263 m long, 38.9 m maximum beam and 10.4 m draft. The correspondence establishes a model comparison datum, not a verified historical loading condition.

The forward hull uses a fine waterline entrance, flared upper stem and separate submerged bulb. The deck descends gradually from ahead of the bridge over approximately 32 m, then rises toward the bow. Aft of x=+76 (bow-positive) the hull stations are fitted to the reference's cross-sections on an even 1.25 m grid and eased into the original bow: a slimmer forward shoulder, the reference's fine V-shaped run with a rising keel, a main deck flat at 8.54/8.40 m back to the stern hangar walls, a step at x=-113.5 to the 5.99 m quarterdeck, a recess under the overhanging deck edge from x=-104.5 to -76.5 and an overhanging cruiser stern. The waterline points amidships are eased 1.7 cm out to hold the documented 36.9 m waterline beam. Flood spaces, reserve cells, belt armor and stability (dry CG at the new buoyancy centre, GM 7% of beam, buoyancy scale reconciling the stated mass with the new volume) follow the changed hull. The bridge has a battered lower foundation, a 6.696 m wide vertical upper core, overhanging lookout galleries with rounded undersides, a raised navigation room with a sawtooth-bracketed window ledge and supported signal wings; 13 mm twins, searchlight control stations, 4.5 m rangefinders, E27 and A6 antennas and an aft annex carrying the after directors stand at the reference positions. The director has an asymmetric faceted housing, aft-set tapered rangefinder arms and a raked aerial. Staggered Type 22 horns stand on supported open upper wings. The funnel has a narrow capsule section with a raked skirt, seven external steam pipes a side, a walkway collar and a grated cap with three domed rain covers, over a deckhouse that carries the searchlights, Type 95 directors and rangefinders at the reference stations; the mainmast is a single raked pole with lattice outriggers and two Type 13 aerials. The shelter deck, its round 12.7 cm towers and walled tubs and all 54 25 mm positions (closed triples on drums, open triples in tubs, singles) follow the reference layout; the reference has no mounts on the main-turret roofs. Control stations have round-cornered hoods with rolled roofs and visors. Ground tackle, aircraft-deck catapults on cone pedestals, the stowed crane, transfer rails, stern anchors, a centreline skeg and screws at the reference positions complete the decks. Gunhouse floors and supporting barbettes are positioned against the reference while retaining their original yaw, elevation, recoil and socket IDs.

## Limits

- Sparse dimensional comparison does not certify every surface or historical detail. Gunhouse shapes, exposed AA mechanisms, exact gallery fittings and aft equipment remain independently interpreted; the reference is more detailed.
- The main battery uses the original 46 cm triple recipe (`assets/parts/ijn-main-guns/type94_460_triple.py`), drawn after the reference's JGM178 visual: the catalog gunhouse mesh is the visible housing (45° face, raked sides, rounded rear, armoured rangefinder hoods), and the trunnion, muzzle reach and 3.05 m bore spacing follow the reference joints. The ship-owned barbettes still reach the shared bearing at Y=2.15 m above the unchanged installed yaw datums, so the roller foot stands 0.25 m taller than the reference's turntable ring. The canvas is procedurally folded and smoother than the reference's. Internal layouts, armor thicknesses, loading, flooding and ballistics remain provisional gameplay models.
- The two 15.5 cm triples use `assets/parts/ijn-main-guns/type3_155_triple.py`, drawn after the reference's JGS156 visual. Twelve twin 127 mm AA mounts use the shielded (outer) and open (inner) Type 89 recipes in `assets/parts/ijn-yamato-aa/`, at the reference hardpoints and 0.652 m barrel spacing, with shared aiming, finite ammunition and damage; their rates, arcs and supply are gameplay approximations. The 25 mm closed, 1945 closed, open and single mounts (`yamato_type96.py`) and the 13 mm fittings remain visual.
- Barrels that pass over neighbours still cross some fixed geometry at the reference positions: the superfiring main turret over the lower one at full depression, the aft 15.5 cm triple over the aft director and the raised 25 mm tubs, and the inner 127 mm mounts over the searchlight tub and the funnel deckhouse (167 blocked poses in the whole-ship sweep, from about 430).
- The funnel deckhouse is visual only, with no structure record or hit surface; the stern hangar well is represented by a hatch because the hull loft closes the deck. The 263 m hull ends at the reference's stern but the waterline length measures 256.95 m against the documented 256 m (it was 257.45 m before the refit).
- Mount movement uses the shared box `obstructions` like every other ship: mounts reach their nominal catalog arcs, and a line of fire crossing an authored obstruction box reports the mount blocked. The former closed-body `mountClearance` swept interlock was removed because its mesh solver dominated the simulation step (see [simulation performance](../../../docs/sim-performance-plan.md)); the retired profile is kept as a solver regression fixture in `src/simulation/fixtures/yamato-swept-clearance.json`, with the hull, structures and mounts it was authored around in `yamato-swept-clearance-ship.json`. Barrels are therefore no longer stopped visually at AA tubs and rangefinders, so [model acceptance check 4](../../../docs/ship-model-review.md) (clearance through traverse, elevation and recoil) remains a visual review of the published model.
- Appearance follows the [shared fleet finish](../../../docs/ship-appearance.md). `appearance.json` retains named paints and deck coverings. It wears In commission, drawn by the game like every ship's (plating, mottling, runoff, tide stain, funnel soot); Blender bakes only fine paint grain. The bare teak weather decks keep the retired procedural teak's stain (its baked mean, `yamato-authored-teak`) and its 0.16 m planks with 5 m butts; the 4 mm seams follow the fleet's teak. The steel strips and deck ends of `yamato_deck.py` stay steel.
