# Shōkaku — corrected island and stern review

The reported window z-fighting, oversized repetitive bridge tiers and incomplete stern arrangement were real defects in the preceding model. They are corrected in the original blueprint/recipe and rebuilt into the playable carrier. The [before-and-after record](correction-02/README.md) supersedes the earlier acceptance of these features.

Exact reviewed content hash: `2d43863548c9dfcee9b1822b75dee70d501d16271e334d13ce5c1accfd00c0b8`. The [export](export.json) contains 339,244 triangles, 183 meshes, 397 primitives and 11,920,880 bytes. The definition, GLB and generated Blender scene share that hash. The tool actually used was **local Blender 5.2.0 LTS**; no Blender MCP tool was exposed.

## Actual game and validation

Chrome/WebGPU loaded the final exported model. Eighteen aircraft across all three Japanese roles [launched](runtime-2d438635/launch.json), [recovered and rearmed](runtime-2d438635/recovery.json), restoring 72 ready aircraft. A mixed Shōkaku/Enterprise/Bismarck fleet loads all six aircraft models at three LODs. [Swept shells and an armed torpedo](runtime-2d438635/damage.json) caused hull, module and flooding damage through normal simulation; no damage state was assigned directly. The normal [return-to-port control](runtime-2d438635/reset.json) restored integrity, modules, ammunition and aircraft. [Armor, internals and flood-space inspection](runtime-2d438635/inspection.json) remains available. Fixture overrides are disclosed in their records and the [reproduction guide](../authoring/README.md).

`bun run test` passes **892 tests across 124 files**, with zero failures. `bun run build` passes fleet ship/aircraft checks, TypeScript and production bundling. The existing shared preload chunk exceeds Vite's 7,000 kB warning threshold; the build succeeds. See [validation](validation.json), [tests](tests.log), [build](build.log) and [sources](../references/sources.json).

Earlier full gameplay evidence for strike payloads, surface fire and automatic AA remains under `runtime-16bd50f5/` and retains its original model hash. This correction changes the ship geometry, hull calibration and inspection proxies; it does not repeat those earlier records under a new hash. Aircraft wings remain fixed because their original rigs lack folding hinges. Historical loading, internal subdivision and service/weapon tuning remain documented approximations.
