# Discrepancy register

The export checks certify the blueprint/model contract, not historical accuracy.

| Item | Status / basis |
| --- | --- |
| Class, five 5-inch guns, ten 21-inch tubes, six projectors and two tracks | Supported by NHHC DANFS Fletcher summary. |
| Length and beam | Rounded from the documented 376 ft 3 in and 39 ft 8 in. The independent hull matches the authored 114.7 × 12.1 m. |
| Loading condition | Open. 4.2 m draft and 2,924 t displacement are provisional loaded gameplay values; the cited summary instead lists 2,100 t and 13 ft without establishing this preset's load. |
| Hull sections / stern / bow | Revision 3: 127 original station rings, a deep raked stem, fuller foredeck, flatter floor, longer afterbody and broad transom. Sparse matching-raster probes and all fixed views were reviewed; see shape-correction.md. Exact lines and station offsets remain open; no full construction lines plan was available. |
| Bridge, stacks, mast, rigging, boats and fittings | Rebuilt from inspected July 1942 photographs, ONI recognition drawings and GameModels3D comparison views. Revision 3 raises and reshapes the forward deckhouse, uses a narrow bridge core with projecting wings at the measured heights, rounds the Mount 52 deck front, and adjusts the after house, funnels, mast, director and boats. Exact dimensions and minor date-specific fittings remain interpreted. |
| Paint / markings | Original Measure-12-inspired camouflage and 445 bow numbers; not a sampled or traced photograph/game texture. Exact colours, pattern boundaries, weathering and differences between port and starboard are not certified. |
| Light AA | One twin Bofors in the high after tub and six Oerlikons (two lower bridge wings, four in the waist), guided by early photos. Later GameModels3D/ONI AA tubs are deliberately excluded. Precise commissioning-day outfit remains open. |
| 5-inch Mk 30 enclosure | Revision 4: original 92-facet enclosure shared with CPU armor, taller sloping roof, shoulder corners, front gun-port recess, curved elevating shield, rear doors, sights and revised barrel/boot. OP 1112 p.288 (Mod 18) and GameModels3D rasters were inspected; exact Mod 0 dimensions and fittings remain interpreted. Protection, reload, penetration and damage are provisional. |
| Propellers and shaft brackets | Revision 4: paired handed 4.2 m-diameter, three-bladed screws with broad rounded lobes, camber, finite thickness, changing pitch, ogival hubs and foil-section brackets seated in our hull. Six blade solids are closed and manifold. Diameter, 3.8 m nominal pitch, detailed sections, shaft spacing and handedness are visual estimates; the Navy photo is a postwar sister ship, not a DD-445 propeller plan. |
| Torpedo mounts | Two independently trained five-tube assemblies. Training rate and safe broadside sectors are game settings. Launches use a small gyro correction, gravity to enter the water, and a fixed running depth. No manual speed settings, homing, dud reliability model or spare torpedoes. |
| Depth-charge armament | Two racks and six side projectors, with original rimmed drums, roller tracks, K-gun cradles and ready-service detail. All stations share a Mk 6-inspired game part. 28 rounds and their distribution (8 per rack, 2 per projector) are tuning. Visible ready-service drums do not disappear as ammunition is spent. |
| Depth-charge performance | Constant 2.5 m/s sink, 10 m burst, 32 m radius, quadratic falloff, six-second station reload and 0.35 s launch spacing are game approximations. They do not reproduce shockwave physics, variable hydrostatic pistols, hull shielding, sonar, diving, or historic damage radii. |
| Blast reach | Uses station half-breadths, keel heights and the submerged vertical hull envelope. Simplified box-like cross-sections overestimate reach near rounded bilges. All teams and the source ship can be damaged. |
| Machinery / armor / subdivisions | Provisional inspectable envelopes. Revision 3 repositions/resizes the steering and after-magazine spaces for the revised afterbody; steering flood capacity is 58 m³. Structural steel at 12.7 mm hull / 6 mm deckhouse and gunhouse resistance are not a verified armor schedule. Exposed ready service has a small game compartment for shared damage/flooding links. |
| Moving fittings | Gun and torpedo mount articulation is functional. Screw and rudder pivots are retained but remain static while sailing. Both screw pivots were manually rotated in the loaded game model for inspection; this is not new sailing animation. |
| GameModels3D | Inspected via seventeen retained raster captures of WoWS Fletcher pasd021. Identical cameras and one global 15 m/viewer-unit registration support raster silhouette comparison; source waterline/refit are unverified. No game topology, transforms, UVs or textures entered the production recipe. |
| Plans access | ONI 222-US page 89 side/top recognition drawings, Navy OP 1112 p.288 mount drawing and All Hands October 1952 p.3 propeller photograph were actually inspected and preserved. Sigsbee and Bath Iron Works full plates could not be retrieved; no dimensional claims are based on those inaccessible plates. |
