# Main turret silhouette correction

The preceding gunhouses had an incorrect sloping front plate and a broad bevel around the roof. Those features made both mountings read as tapered boxes.

Revision 3 uses the retained RN gunhouse plan (plate 2), mounting sections (plate 62), IWM A3655 and A3905, and the April 1940 Mk III construction photograph. The original geometry has upright front plates close to the existing trunnions, continuous sloping sides, a rounded rear outline and shallow fore/aft roof planes. Quadruple and twin roofs are separate interpretations. Broad flared rangefinder covers replace detached optical boxes; rounded-bottom elevation slots, sight apertures and the forward D-shaped sill complete the face.

Each before/after pair uses the complete raster from the same orthographic camera and metric scale. `compose-review.py` reproduces the sheets after `ship:build`; there is no component registration or nonuniform scaling. The previous source, catalog, published model and camera manifest are retained in `before/`.

The edits live in `author-blueprint.py`, the two original 14-inch catalog entries and `build.py`. Assembly, train/elevation/recoil joint and muzzle IDs remain unchanged. Gunhouse armor follows the revised closed catalog enclosure; recessed ports remain visual detail over that simplified closed CPU envelope.

The plan and section drawings establish the form, not an exact coordinate survey. Roof pitch, local dimensions, rangefinder covers and minor fittings remain inferred. Sister-ship photographs support mounting construction but do not verify King George V’s complete early-1941 outfit. GameModels3D is a raster comparison only.
