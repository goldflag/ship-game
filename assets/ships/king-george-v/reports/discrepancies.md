# King George V discrepancy register

Configuration: early 1941 Home Fleet exterior, before December AA changes. Waterline: separately declared 1940 standard mean-draft datum. Intended use: playable historical preset with documented game approximations.

| ID | Status | Evidence and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KGV-001 | Dimensions measured | Actual exported triangles are checked for length, beam, mean keel draft, waterline length and midship depth. See dimensions.json. Numerical agreement with authored targets is not the uncertainty of the historical records. |
| KGV-002 | Source conflict open | RMG B9's 112 ft 4.25 in maximum beam conflicts with the builder-model record's 103 ft and its own approximately 103 ft waterline beam. The bare hull uses 103 ft; projecting secondary barrels exceed it. The difference is not silently treated as a recovered hull dimension. |
| KGV-003 | Loading explicit | RMG B9 lists 29 ft standard and 32 ft 6 in deep draft. The game uses the former, with the guide's standard displacement. This does not reproduce King George V's precise May 1941 fuel, stores, displacement or trim. The quoted waterline length is imposed at the authored datum. |
| KGV-004 | Body-plan interpretation improved; offsets open | Revision 2 uses 228 stations and 21 half-section points from an original manual reading of the 4800 px Vickers NPB5265 body plan. It replaces the generic rounded loft with finer entry, flare, bilges and a rising stern. The 1937/38 design drawing predates the 1941 fit; ordinate origin, interpolation and scan readings remain approximate. This is not a recovered builder offset table. |
| KGV-005 | Local proportions measured; source disagreement open | Revision 4 corrects the 24% short twin body and 11% short quad body against fixed GameModels3D raster spans. Original catalog lengths are now 11.9 m twin and 13.2 m quad; lower, narrower roofs, an aft quad crown, longer jacket sleeves, shorter exposed barrels and roof-opening scallops follow independently authored geometry. B's barbette and the tower forefoot clearance are updated. Seven retained pixel measurements compare before/reference/after; see visual-iteration-04/measurements.json. RN plate 62 supports the near-equal twin/quad lengths, but the RN plan and Vickers profile suggest roughly 12.7–12.8 m quad length and a taller crown than the game raster. This revision follows the local game comparison; it does not resolve those historical differences. The game deck also lies about 1.2 m lower in the common cameras. Load datum, exact side slopes, barrel contours, UP projectors and small fittings remain approximate. All 26 barrel chains and stable mount/socket IDs survive; CPU gunhouse armor retains closed simplified face/roof envelopes behind the visible ports. |
| KGV-006 | Bridge/funnels rebuilt; local fit open | NPB5316 and the full-resolution profile guide the distinct tower trunk, projecting admiral/navigation cabins, signal wings, HACS seating and mast platforms. Open funnel jackets, arched cap lattice, searchlight walks, steam pipes and service ladders replace plain stacks. Local dimensions remain about 1–1.5 m interpretations; exact service galleries and every aperture are not verified. |
| KGV-007 | Early AA represented visually; stations provisional | Four octuple pom-poms and four UP projectors retained. B-turret UP is supported by IWM A3655. The revised two aft-turret / one quarterdeck projector arrangement remains provisional pending a fully legible dated AA plan. Legacy up-waist assembly IDs survive the placement revision. Later Oerlikon/Bofors/Type 271 additions visible in the game reference are excluded. |
| KGV-008 | Secondary performance qualified | RMG B9's isolated six-gun count is a typo relative to its eight twin mounts and the plan. Use sixteen bores. Its 80° elevation conflicts with the Mk I technical summary; use −5°/+70°. RMG muzzle velocity is retained. SAP uses the shared AP solver; HE fraction, fuze/fragment behavior, penetration, dispersion and reload are game approximations. |
| KGV-009 | Protection approximate | Nominal 373/349 mm belt, 149/124 mm decks, 324 mm faces, 225 mm sides, 174 mm rear, 149 mm roof and 25 mm secondary protection follow a published summary. A primary armor plan is still missing. Belt vertical registration, three-step taper, deck extensions, constant barbette thickness and bulkhead shapes are estimated. Composition/backing, splinter-deck layers, local thickness changes, detailed conning protection and torpedo-defense compartments remain omitted. |
| KGV-010 | Internals refitted; hydrostatics calibrated | Eight boilers and four turbine groups retain IDs, stations and relationships. Five original room envelopes were reduced to fit the new hull; 152 spaces, closed boundaries and residual cells were regenerated. Geometric displacement is about 31,177 t at the adopted draft versus 38,641 t stated mass; buoyancy scale 1.2394 and estimated CG reconcile the game equilibrium. This mismatch is unresolved historical evidence, not a certified displacement curve. |
| KGV-011 | Fittings refined; operations simplified | Open-well boat hulls, motor-launch cabins, Carley floats, deck gear, capstans, anchor links, crane lattice/cabins and transverse catapult are independently authored. Walrus, exact boat outfit, shaft exits, blade pitch and minor fittings remain approximate. Stable crane/director/propeller/rudder empties survive, but these appendages remain static in game. |
| KGV-012 | Finish approximate | Plain Home Fleet gray, teak, boot topping, anti-fouling and White Ensign are original materials/geometry. Exact Admiralty paint reflectance, weathering, scuttle locations and markings are not colorimetric or frame-by-frame reconstructions. |
| KGV-013 | Mechanical failures omitted | Early-war turret jamming and crew drill failures are not simulated. Catalog train, elevation and nominal reload govern the mounts. No claim of reproducing the Bismarck action's gun availability. |
| KGV-014 | Validation recorded separately | Export, decoded measurements, tests and browser checks are tied to hashes in the validation record. These establish playable integration and selected dimensional checks, not blanket historical accuracy. |
| KGV-015 | Independent authoring tested | GameModels3D pbsb107 stock hull is an isolated raster reference, not historical ground truth. Raw game geometry, transforms, UVs and textures never enter authoring. Thirty fixed cameras compare the original GLB. The complete production build is checked with the raw reference cache unavailable; see independence.json and the authoring read audit. |

## Local damage and fire calibration — 2026-09-06

Local regions, generator and director proxies, combustible loads and smoke outlets are independently authored gameplay estimates from the existing layout. They are not historically measured structural subdivisions, generator schedules or ventilation plans. Electrical supply is aggregated with manual gun fallback; directors share a targeting penalty. Breach overlap uses bounded aperture sampling. See `assets/ships/author-local-damage.ts`.
