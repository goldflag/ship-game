# Type VIIC discrepancies and scope

The export checks establish dimensions, sockets and hierarchy, not historical accuracy. The exterior now targets U-570 as captured in August–September 1941. Primary dimensions, a uniformly registered captured plan and survey photographs replace the earlier generic envelope. It remains an independently reconstructed game asset, with the unresolved items below.

## Evidence conflicts retained

- The British survey gives 20 ft 2¼ in beam (6.15315 m); the captured notebook gives 6.18 m and nominal class summaries round to 6.2 m. The model uses the dimensioned British survey, without claiming that the other load/build conventions are erroneous.
- Surface draft and displacement depend on tank contents. The 4.7625 m draft is a declared reference datum. The existing 769,000 kg game mass, 220 m² waterplane and 160 m³ reserve buoyancy are not a reproduction of the British 739/784 long-ton loading cases.
- British section pressure diameter is 15 ft 6 in (4.7244 m), while metric class/notebook data says 4.70 m. The body uses the dimensioned section, with this difference recorded.
- The general arrangement has slight local scale/skew differences: profile registration has one uniform 68.4645 px/m scale, not separate height/beam fits. Intermediate outlines have approximately 0.15–0.3 m interpretation uncertainty. A registered overlay exposes disagreement; it does not certify a shape match.
- Scope extension differs by drawing/photo. The raised attack eye uses the notebook maximum 14.612 m above keel, with the 4.7625 m reference draft subtracted. The operating 7 m depth order remains gameplay tuning.
- The C/30 fixed pedestal was raised in iteration 2 after comparing its barrel axis with the captured profile. Shared gun receivers, sights, seats and ammunition feed remain simplified. Torpedo shutters are unanimated dark apertures, not a detailed door mechanism.
