Numbered PDF pages above are one-based; raster filenames ending in a number use zero-based PDF indices. The 1800-pixel sheet rasters and detail crops are inspection derivatives of these PDFs, not replacement sources.

Cobalt's cover photograph is **1944**, whereas the drawings are **1941**. The cover, `cobalt-underway-model-monkey.jpeg` and the earlier Sackville museum photo are not evidence for the selected fit. `flower-1939-1940-top-jproc.jpg` is a generic early Flower diagram, and `cobalt-bridge-plan-jproc.jpg` a supplementary crop from Jerry Proc's radio-room research; the full builder scan takes precedence. Context URLs: https://www.jproc.ca/rrp/corvette.html and https://www.model-monkey.com/product-page/general-arrangement-plans-flower-class-hmcs-cobalt-k124.

The collier profile is a postwar drawing by Karsten-Kunibert Krueger-Kopiske, not a builder's schematic. Its reproduced launch photograph is dated 18 December 1944. No full original collier GA was located: hatch/house measurements and wartime gun inventory remain explicitly provisional. ABS also explains why the Liberty tanker is unsuitable as a visibly different third silhouette: it was intentionally disguised as the cargo type.

All meshes are independently authored. Reference copies remain under assets for research, with no implication that all source material is public-domain or suitable for redistribution. No scan is used as a game texture.
