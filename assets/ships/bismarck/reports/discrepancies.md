The preserved first-iteration sheets in `visual-iteration-01/` provide a fixed comparison for this detail pass.

## Master integration: exterior protection presentation

The reconstructed belt envelopes can sit about 1.1 m inside the faired visual hull at midship. Combat and inspection use a shared 1.5 m replacement allowance for nearby exterior armor. Nominal hull skin is clipped out of those inspection patches so the researched belt remains visible and pickable; unarmored hull ends retain their ordinary-steel surface. This is a reconciliation of approximate authored envelopes, not a newly verified historical plating offset. Mixed-fleet collision and sight aiming use each actor's own definition.
