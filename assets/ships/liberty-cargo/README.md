# Liberty Cargo — EC2-S-C1

A standard five-hold Liberty, reconstructed from USMC/Gibbs & Cox drawing 312-S1-3-2 (30 August 1941) and Thomas Sully's as-built midship section (28 August 1943). The source's molded beam is 56 ft 10¼ in, replacing the earlier rounded 56-foot estimate. A flat merchant floor and tight bilge replace the generic rounded hull.

The amidship engine/deckhouse group, three single cargo masts, five hatches, four lifeboats and aft guard quarters are structural features, with no cosmetic cargo-load variant. Representative defensive battery: aft 5-inch, forward 3-inch and eight Oerlikons; 11 kn, two boilers and single reciprocating drive/shaft.

Dimensions: 134.5692 × 17.32915 m; chosen loaded draft 8.4328 m. Fine end sections, load state, fittings and defensive battery are interpreted, not a certified Thomas Sully reconstruction.
