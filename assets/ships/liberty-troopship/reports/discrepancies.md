# Discrepancy register

- Open — Hull sections, stem/counter curvature, sheer, deckhouse placement and equipment are original interpreted geometry. Fixed render views check this authored target; no offsets plan has been measured.
- Open — Published Liberty beam varies between 56 ft (NPS) and 57 ft (John W. Brown). The preset uses 56 ft; length is 441 ft 6 in. The 8.46 m loaded draft and 14,478 t mass are a declared approximate load, not an exact paired historical measurement.
- Open — Paint, hatch covers, rigging leads, cargo tie-downs and auxiliary fittings are representative. General cargo, vehicle load and limited troop transport are distinct game fits, not exact dated manifests.
- Open — Ordinary steel shell and deckhouse thicknesses are estimated. There is no armored citadel. Gun protection uses catalog proxies; the Flower shield is open-backed visually while the simulation uses the shared nominal gunhouse proxy.
- Open — Room envelopes, permeability, bulkheads, pumps, module health/immersion limits, dry CG, GM and uniform buoyancy calibration are gameplay approximations. See stability.json. They are neither certified watertight plans nor historical stability curves.
- Open — Gun ballistics, AP/HE stocks, traverse/elevation rates, reload, armor resistance and damage are provisional. Small-caliber guns engage surface targets; aircraft operations remain absent.
- Open — Cargo, shelters, derricks, rigging, boats, screw and rudder are visual fittings. No crane operation, loading/unloading or troop objectives are implemented.
- Reviewed — Every functional gun retains independent yaw, elevation, recoil and muzzle sockets. Source and runtime coordinate conversion are checked by the shared export validator. Propeller and rudder pivot empties remain independent.
