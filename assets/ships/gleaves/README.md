# Hsienyang — Gleaves-class destroyer

## Approved brief

Approved by the user on 2026-09-08: reproduce **Hsienyang (ex-USS Rodman), Hull A** from [GameModels3D, vehicle pzsd108](https://gamemodels3d.com/games/worldofwarships/vehicles/pzsd108), using **GameModels3D-only** external visual references.

- **Configuration:** stock Hsienyang (A), `PZUH702_D8_HULL_A`; four single 127 mm/38 Mk.12 guns on Mk.30 mod.0 mounts, two quintuple 533 mm Mk14 torpedo banks, six single Mk4 and two twin Mk24 20 mm Oerlikons, and the source's fitted directors, radar and depth-charge equipment.
- **Appearance:** the shown default weathered blue-grey vertical surfaces, dark reddish-brown decks, dark funnel caps and rust-red bottom. No added hull numbers, camouflage or cloth flags absent from the approved source.
- **Date limitation:** Hsienyang is the ship's post-transfer identity; the source's underlying asset name includes “1945.” The exact historical refit date is unverified and that limitation is accepted. This preset targets fidelity to the approved source model, not independently certified historical accuracy.
- **Authoring:** geometry and textures are independently authored. Source meshes/textures are inspection-only and never enter the recipe or runtime asset. Gameplay and internal arrangements use provisional game conventions where the visual reference supplies no evidence.
- **Sensors:** the approved A-model's SC2 and SG search radars use separate rotating joints on modeled mast platforms. Their 6/15 RPM visual animation follows existing game conventions; these are not verified historical operating rates.
- **Technical-reference exception (2026-09-09):** the user approved consulting technical references for gun travel stops after the upper forward gun collided with the lower gunhouse at −15° depression. This exception does not broaden the visual reference policy. [NAVPERS 10111, Mk30 manual](https://www.eugeneleeslover.com/US-NAVY-GUNS/Navpers_10111.php) and the [1943 Navy gun-crew instructions](https://www.maritime.org/doc/destroyer/fiveinch/index.php) are the starting references; installed Hsienyang travel limits remain unverified. A firing cutout alone is not a mechanical clearance solution.

These clearance-derived approximations are applied in the shared blueprint contract; they are not verified Hsienyang stop-cam profiles. Catalog family capabilities remain separate so installed limits do not split weapon selection groups.

### Accuracy pass against pzsd108 (2026-09-24)

- **Hull.** The loft is 80 sections of 20 points measured from the reference shell: a centreline fin point pair carries the stem bar and the skeg (full draught to 37.6 m, trailing edge to 39 m), then the body outline by arc length below 1.8 m and by height above it. Bilge keels, shafts, struts, the rudder and the sonar dome are excluded from the loft and modelled as fittings. The waterline now runs 51.97 m forward to 52.0 m aft like the reference; the length stays 106.4 m, so the hull closes with a zero-width run between the transom head (52.5 m) and the stern end. Beam 10.94 m, draft 3.92 m. The forecastle sheer and break follow the reference deck line. Geometric displacement at the reference waterline is 2,287 t against the stated 2,400 t: flood spaces and then stability were re-derived (buoyancy scale 1.049, dry CG 0, 0.53, 2.07). Rooms whose bottom corners the rising floor cut were raised or narrowed with capacity kept at 72% of the box.
- **Handling.** The measured afterbody has much less lateral area than the old full-draught loft, so the inferred-rudder calibration input `handling.maxYawRate` drops from 0.033 to 0.028. With that change the trial turns at 3.00°/s, against 2.73°/s on master.
- **Structures.** The machinery casing is 2.60 m in half-breadth aft of 7.4 m and 1.62 m forward of it, with a 2.12 m block up to the break; its 4.80 m roof is flush with a walkway that continues the forecastle deck over the break. The forward deckhouse has the reference's flat after face and chamfered front. The funnels are round 1.19 m shells on flared plinths, with caps at 13.95 m and 12.80 m. The 01 level continues aft as an open platform with two lockers. The bridge-wing screens are 1.52 m with a weather lip, and the after lookout platforms stand on plated lockers.
- **Fittings.** The main mounts sit at the reference hardpoints plus 0.17 m, the height at which the shared Mk30 mod.0 gunhouse matches the reference's roof and skirt. The 26 ft whaleboats are stowed forward and high between crescent davits. The foremast stands on the trunk roof. The aft pole is at 19.0 m with a topmast and a fore-and-aft spar. The SC2 reflector and the aerial staff follow the reference, and the torpedo tubes are 8.08 m long with the aft trainer's cab riding on them. The screws are Ø2.72 m at 46.35 m on the measured shaft line, with one strut and an A-bracket each, and the rudder is the reference's large blade. The stern roller-track frames stand 1.65 m high.
- **Accepted differences.** The reference's torpedo-handling telphers and stern smoke generators are not part of this preset's fit and are left out. The upper mast ladder stays aft of the mast (the reference carries it on the port side) because a forward ladder fouls the SG reflector. The shared Mk30 gunhouse is about 0.3 m longer at the rear than the reference's, and its skirt hangs lower; it was measured but not edited. There is no deck-edge rail round gun 3, whose gunhouse overhangs the 3.12 m deck edge abeam, and the perimeter rail has openings where guns 1 and 4 depress abeam and where the after single Oerlikons train aft. The stem head ends 0.1 m short of the reference's.
