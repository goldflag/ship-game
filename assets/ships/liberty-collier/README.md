# Liberty Collier — EC2-S-AW1

A genuinely different Liberty-family design: the machinery and long accommodation poop move aft; the navigation bridge is detached forward; five coal holds have ten hinged steel hatch covers and lifting posts instead of cargo derricks. Boilers are represented on the raised aft platform described by ABS. The unladen structural silhouette differs from the general cargo ship.

Evidence: ABS's *Workhorse of the Fleet*, printed p.105; a reproduced class profile; Jagger Seam underway and Delta Shipbuilding's launch photograph dated 18 December 1944. The profile depicts a postwar Mystic ship and is not a builder drawing; its funnel logo is not copied. A complete original collier GA/body plan has not been obtained.

Representative armed 1945 fit: aft 5-inch, forward 3-inch and six Oerlikons, 11 kn and one reciprocating drive. Exact gun inventory, hatch/house dimensions, draft and displacement remain estimates. Dimensions/loading are explicitly stated in the blueprint rather than asserted as a named ship's certified condition.
