# Exported aircraft schematic review

All 13 final aircraft GLBs were projected independently onto the retained drawings and all 26 side/top overlays were visually inspected. Reviewed recipe: `a6c5b1fd623c6a92224f3295ab05b19bb2b4e58e51d3551b886315003bae4844`.

The rebuilt principal outlines are suitable for this exterior game-asset pass. Cowling taper, concave fin roots, the Val wheel fairings, the Judy radiator, and cockpit containment now follow the measured reference envelopes. SBD and SB2C brake extents fit their wing outlines. The Corsair exported wing-height samples retain the measured gull break and rising outer panels.

All wingspans match the registered target dimensions. Full exported length differs from the target by at most 1.22%. Maximum sampled wing leading/trailing-edge residual is 0.0292 m. These residuals verify the measured station interpretation; they do not certify every contour or historical detail.
