# Aircraft historical and visual discrepancy register

Updated 5 September 2026 for the second authoring pass. This collection contains original visual assets for future carrier and air-combat work. It spans early and late WWII; it does not represent one carrier air group, battle or date. All thirteen aircraft now have individually measured shape specifications and retained drawing evidence. Export checks establish agreement with authored targets, not historical accuracy.

The earlier catalog, recipe and overview sheets remain in [baseline-v1](../baseline-v1/). Statements in earlier validation records apply to the hashes and images named there. Current rebuild, fixed-view review, comparison and browser results belong in the [validation record](validation.md); this document does not declare those checks complete.

## Current evidence and interpretation

[Shape specifications](../shapes/) record the actual source image, attribution, variant, pixel datums and manually sampled exterior contours. [Retained schematics](../references/schematics/) include primary Navy and manufacturer/archive drawings as well as credited published technical drawings. Source resolution and provenance differ by aircraft. These are independently constructed meshes guided by measured silhouettes; no external 3D model topology, UV maps or textures are imported.

The [independent comparator](../../../scripts/aircraft/compare.py) decodes the actual exported GLBs and projects their geometry over fixed drawing datums. Per-aircraft `generated/comparison/` contains side/top overlays and hashed measurements; [schematic-comparisons.json](schematic-comparisons.json) indexes the collection. The method does not fit a model silhouette to the drawing. Its residuals can reveal export or modeling discrepancies, but cannot establish that a published drawing or cross-variant inference is correct. A report marked **ready for visual review** is not a visual or historical approval.

## Implementation limits

- Wings remain spread, with no fold joints or deck-storage poses. Aileron controls are not fold mechanisms; the SBD has no wing fold.
- Neutral assets use an approximately level engine shaft and extended gear, rather than a physically supported tail-down deck stance. Main/tail gear retraction, hinge alignment and linkage clearance are approximate inspection motions.
- Transparent glazing and cockpit interiors are now modeled. Crew, exact instruments, canopy opening mechanisms, factory cross-sections and complete interior structure are absent.
- SBD/Helldiver split brakes now use real apertures in thin upper and lower plates, with separate `diveBrake.port` / `.starboard` and `diveBrake.lower.port` / `.starboard` joints. Hole density is reduced for the mesh budget. These independent plates do not certify deployment linkage geometry or aerodynamic behavior.
- Payload provision is attachment sockets and selected exterior bay indications. Carried bombs, torpedoes, operational load limits, release mechanisms and full working bays remain absent.
- Main control surfaces, propellers, gear, hooks, the Avenger turret and selected rear gun mounts retain independent pivots. Visual gun barrels do not implement weapons or combat poses.
- Three LOD meshes retain the articulated owner hierarchy. Their decimation targets and suggested switch distances require visual comparison and future fleet profiling; successful budget checks alone are not a performance guarantee.
- Many production details remain simplified, including intake/exhaust internals, canopy framing, guns, aerials, skin fasteners and gear doors. The measured silhouette is a stronger reference basis, not a complete manufacturing reconstruction.

## Review status

The V2 workflow produces six fixed views per aircraft and independently registered side/top GLB overlays. During refinement, `compare.py --allow-stale` may create explicitly labeled diagnostic comparisons; these must not be presented as current validation evidence. After the final rebuild, regenerate comparisons and overview sheets, inspect all views and articulated parts, verify LOD changes and transparency in the browser, and record the checked hashes and observations in [validation.md](validation.md).

Keep source uncertainty separate from model defects. A passing dimension or export check does not close a historical item above; a visible deviation from an accepted drawing should remain open until corrected or explicitly justified.
