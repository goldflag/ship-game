About this review

The recordings use the actual ship, articulated guns, CPU shell trajectories, ocean and effects. Frames are encoded at fixed simulation timestamps for smooth playback, even when the preview window is in the background. The artifact contains its own videos, images and fonts. These recordings precede the latest Bismarck model and daylight updates; the current integration passes the checks shown here.

The smoke takes inspiration from the vendored Sky Pro clouds: 3D density, eroded edges and light absorption. Its slower motion follows the broad, cohesive plumes in the supplied New Jersey firing footage. Fire timing, smoke density and spray speeds are gameplay approximations. The footage and the US Navy's Iowa broadside photograph are visual references, not a historical certification of Bismarck.
